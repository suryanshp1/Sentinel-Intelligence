# Code of Conduct

Sentinel welcomes participation regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to help make collaboration respectful and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code respectfully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and explain actionable feedback.
- Respect differing experience, viewpoints, and reasonable boundaries.
- Give credit, preserve attribution, and respect privacy.
- Accept corrections and focus on resolving the issue.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable. Repeated disruption, spam, and intimidation are also unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact a repository maintainer privately using an available contact channel. If none is published, request a private contact without describing the incident or naming affected people in a public thread. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove content, close or lock discussions, warn participants, restrict participation, or ban accounts according to the severity and pattern of conduct. They should limit disclosure of reports to what is needed to investigate and respond. Maintainers are expected to follow this policy themselves.

This policy applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed spaces, as well as when someone is representing the project publicly. Repository operators should publish a private reporting contact before inviting a public community.
